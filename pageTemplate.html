Observed rank is the observed record a plausible draw from the runs?

Each mark is one ELF: the share of its 20 runs that the observed record is above. If the model is calibrated, the marks spread evenly from 0% to 100%. A pile-up at the right means observed sits in the upper tail of what the model simulates, i.e. the model runs low.
