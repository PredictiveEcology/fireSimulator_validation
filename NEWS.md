# fireSimulator_validation (development version)

* New table "Climate slope by channel": which of the three parts of the climate response carries the difference between the simulated and the observed slope. log(annual area) = log(ignitions) + log(escape rate) + log(mean escaped-fire size) (escapes = fires of the escape size or more, area = `area50`), so the slope of log area on the standardised covariate is the sum of three slopes. `scoreSlopeSplit()` gives, per ELF, the observed slope and, per run, the slope of each part over the same years (the scored years with an ignition, an escape and some area; a year with no escape is left out of all three parts, so they add up exactly to the total slope on those years), reported as simulated (median of runs, single-run range), observed and the difference sim - obs with the observed rank; `slopeSplitTable()` adds the median over ELFs with its 2.5-97.5% range over ELFs (`elfRange()`). `runWave1()` returns it as `slopeSplit` and adds its rows to `results` (test `C`, `null` = `slopeIgnitions`, `slopeEscapeRate`, `slopeSize`, measure `difference`); `buildValidationPage()` has a new argument `slopeSplit` and the page a sortable table under the climate table, with a note that the three differences add up to the total slope difference. The covariate, years and slope helpers are the climate table's (`climateInputs()`, `logSlope()`, `withPooled()`).

* The climate `slope` is reported in its own units instead of as sim / obs, which is unstable when the observed slope is near 0 (on the 47-ELF national score: -3.7 to 13.4, 3 negative ELFs). Per ELF the climate table has the simulated slope (median over runs, 2.5-97.5% range of single runs: new columns `simLo`, `simHi`), the observed slope and the difference sim - obs (`ratio` column: median over runs of the per-run difference, with the single-run range); an observed slope of 0 now gives a finite difference. `obsRank` of the slope is the share of runs with a slope below the observed (`runSummary()` has a new argument `at`, the value that means the observed: 1 for ratios, 0 for the difference). The pooled slope row is the median over ELFs of the difference. In `wave1_results.csv` the slope rows of test `C` have measure `difference`; `dry10` and `wetHalf` stay ratios. The page's climate table shows Simulated slope, Observed slope, Difference (sim - obs), Driest 10%, Wetter half.

* The pooled range over ELFs (bias and climate tables, `elfLo`/`elfHi`, `meanLo`/`meanHi`, and the `lo`/`hi` of pooled rows in `wave1_results.csv`) is now the 2.5-97.5% quantiles over ELFs (`elfRange()`, used by `poolRatios()`), as the page's notes say; it was the minimum and maximum, so one ELF with a wild value set it. Pooled medians are unchanged.

* New climate-response metrics, scored for every arm by the same scorer: `scoreClimateResponse()` gives, per ELF and per run, the slope of log annual area burned on the standardised climate covariate (`slope`), the area in the driest 10% of the scored years (`dry10`) and in the wetter half (`wetHalf`), each as simulated over observed (the definitions of the extreme-fire-years climate comparison). Area is `area50` (fires of the escape size or more, as the page's area row); `dry10` is the years at or above the 90% quantile of the climate covariate over the scored years (about 4 of 39); the covariate is standardised over the scored years, so the observed slope is per standard deviation of the scored years; a year a run lacks counts as no area. The ratio is the median of the single runs, with their 2.5-97.5% range (`runLo`, `runHi`) and the observed rank (`obsRank`, the page's "obs N%"), from `runSummary()`, which `bootRuns()` now calls too. `climateTable()` adds the median over ELFs with the range over ELFs. `runWave1()` returns it as `climate` (computed in the cached `init`) and adds its rows to `results` (test `C`, the metric in `null`, measure `ratio`), so `wave1_results.csv` has them; `validation.html` has a sortable climate-response table. ELFs without a covariate are left out. Gates and scores are unchanged; the `runWave1()` result has one more element and `buildValidationPage()` one more argument. The pooling over ELFs, the long-table rows and the page cells are shared with the bias table (`poolRatios()`, `longRows()`, `rangeCell()`; one `sortableTable()` in the page); the bias results are unchanged.

* The page (`validation.html`), the figures, `wave1.rds`, `wave1_results.csv` and `wave1_bySet.csv` are written by a new event `writeOutputs` from `validationResults`, not by `init`. A cached `init` restores only `validationResults`, so a score job that hit the cache left these files unwritten or stale. Modes `anova` and `weights` are split the same way (new output `weightsResults`), and `scoreBySet()` runs in `init` (new output `bySetResults`), so `writeOutputs` only renders the figures and the page and writes files. `.inputObjects` reads the files of the `anova` and `weights` modes (new inputs `anovaLong`, `weightsTables`), so their content is in `init`'s cache key; a changed file is a miss, as for the hindcast. `runAnova()` and `runWeights()` are removed (`readAnovaScores()`/`computeAnova()`/`writeAnova()`, `readWeightsTables()`/`tuneWeights()`/`writeWeights()`). The page and plot parameters (`pageTitle`, `pageSubtitle`, `pageDpi`, `simLabel`, `.plots`, `.plotInitialTime`) and the input-file parameters of the two modes are no longer in `init`'s cache key; no figure is built when `.plots` is `NA`. A user's `.neverCache` can no longer drop `writeOutputs`. `writeOutputs` is in the default of `.neverCache`; the scoring stays cached.

* Mode `score` reads each finished ELF's hindcast once: `.inputObjects` now calls `readHindcastELFs()`, a `reproducible::Cache()` of `readFireSenseHindcast()` per ELF in `<outputPath>/elfs`, keyed on the ELF's arguments, the path, size and modification time of the files it reads (`hindcastReadFiles()`) and the code of the reader and its callees. A score job reads only the ELFs not read before or whose files changed (every job used to re-read all of them, for hours, with memory growing with the ELF count). A read that fails (error, or a forked worker that died, e.g. out of memory) stops the job naming the ELF and the error; it used to return NULL and the ELF was dropped from the score without a message. `reproducible` is now in `reqdPkgs`.

* `hindcastPath`, `covariatePath`, `inputsPath` and `nCores` are no longer part of the module's event cache keys (`defineParameter(cacheKey = FALSE)`; SpaDES.core >= 3.2.1.9047), so a cache built on one machine is a hit on another where the folders differ. Only `.inputObjects`, which is never cached, reads the folders, and what it read (`validationELFs`) is in the key of `init`. The event cache keys of this module change once.
* New `mode = "anova"` (default `"score"`, unchanged): partitions the variation of the per-ELF skill scores of several arms among ELF, the arms' factors and their interactions. Inputs: score folders (`anovaScores`, each one arm, `wave1_results.csv` as `mode = "score"` writes it; folders with one arm name are combined, e.g. the ELFs of one arm scored apart), a factor table (`anovaFactors`: csv or data.frame, `arm` and one column per factor) and the metrics (`anovaMetrics`, default every `*_N1_skill`). `anovaTerms()` fits `value ~ ELF + factors + ELF:factor + factor:factor` with sum contrasts and reports per term the degrees of freedom, the percentage of the total sum of squares (sequential), the drop-one percentage (for unbalanced designs), F and p; a term aliased with earlier terms (constant factor, two factors that vary together, a cell no arm holds) is reported with a note, never dropped silently. The score folders hold no scores of single runs (only per-year losses and run ranges of ratios), so there is one value per arm x ELF and the residual is the interactions left out of the model, not run noise; the note says so. `runAnova()` writes `anova_terms.csv` and `anova_cells.csv` (medians per ELF x factor levels) and the module draws the stacked percentages through `Plots()`. New output `anovaResults`.

* New bias table: `scoreBias()` gives, for annual area burned, the median of the 10 largest fires, ignitions and escapes, the median run over the observation as a number (`ratio`, `log2ratio`), with the single-run 2.5-97.5% range (`runLo`, `runHi`), the bootstrap interval of the median-run ratio (`lo`, `hi`), the observed rank among runs (`obsRank`) and, for the totals, the implied 1000-year difference (`per1000yr`: ha, ignitions or escapes). The totals (area, ignitions, escapes) also get a mean-run ratio beside the median-run ratio (`meanRatio`: the mean over runs of the run's total over the observed, `meanLo`/`meanHi` its bootstrap interval, `simMean`; pooled: the median over ELFs of `meanRatio` and `meanRatioOfSums`); the mean is what compounds over a long run, the median stays the robust centre. B rows have measure `meanRatio`, and the page shows both. `totalRatio()` returns the mean too (G2 reads only its first two values). With nothing observed the ratio is 1 (nothing simulated) or Inf, never NaN. `poolBias()` pools ELFs two ways: the median of the ELF ratios with their range over ELFs (`elfLo`, `elfHi`), and the ratio of sums (`ratioOfSums`; the sum of the per-ELF medians over the summed observation); ELFs whose ratio is not finite are left out of the median and range and counted (`nELF`, `nNonFinite`). `runWave1()` returns it as `bias` and adds its rows to `results` (test `B`, the quantity in `null`, measure `ratio` or `log2ratio`, `lo`/`hi` the single-run range, or the range over ELFs for pooled rows), so `wave1_results.csv` has them; `validation.html` has a sortable bias table. The area and 10-largest ratios are those of G2 and G4 (same code, `totalRatio()` is now shared with `gateG2()`; the gates are unchanged). Gates, scores and event cache keys are otherwise unchanged; the `runWave1()` result has one more element.

* New parameter `covariatePath` (default `NA`: `hindcastPath`, as before): the folder of hindcasts the climate covariate is read from. A replay hindcast saves no climate, so its score reads its reference hindcast's. `readHindcastCovariate()` now stops naming the missing file.

* New `mode = "weights"` (default `"score"`, unchanged): picks objective weights for the spread fit. Inputs: terms files (`weightsTerms`: the `spreadFitTerms_<runName>.csv` of each ELF as fireSense_spreadFit writes them, with the ELF read from the file name or from the names of the vector, or one table with an `ELF` column; `member` must be the `paramSet` of the bySet scores, and a member with no bySet row stops the run; replicates (`rep`) are reduced to their median; NA terms stop, a NA loss is left out with a warning), the bySet scores of the same members (`weightsBySet`, default `wave1_bySet.csv` in `outputPath`) and a grid of at most 50 weight vectors fixed in advance (`weightsGrid`). `tuneWeights()` takes, per weight vector and ELF, the member that minimises the weighted sum of its terms and reads that member's bySet row (medians and single-run 2.5-97.5% ranges). The vector with the lowest median `weightsLoss` on the tune ELFs (`weightsTuneELFs`) is selected, and only its rows on the held-out ELFs (`weightsReportELFs`, or each ELF in turn with `weightsLeaveOneOut`) are the result, `weights_report.csv`; tune rows are written apart, labelled, in `weights_tuning.csv` and `weights_losses.csv`. `weightsTuneBySet` selects on scores of other years. `runWeights()` reads the files and writes the tables (`readTermsFiles()` reads the terms files). The new parameters change the module's event cache keys once; scoring is otherwise unchanged.

* New parameter `bySet` (default `FALSE`): scores each parameter set of each ELF on its own and writes `wave1_bySet.csv`, one row per set x ELF: the gate ratios (area, median of the 10 largest fires, ignitions, escape rate, area per escape) with single-run 2.5-97.5% ranges, the annual-area skill against N1 and N2, and the G2/G4/G6 results (`scoreBySet()`, which calls `runGates()`, `summariseGates()`, `scoreS3()`). `readHindcastSim()` now reads the `paramSet` column of `hindcast_reps.csv` and `hindcast_sizes.csv` when present (a run is `paramSet * 1000 + rep`) and returns it as `paramSet`; without the column it is the set folder, as before.

* `readHindcastCovariate()` finds the climate file in `<hindcastDir>/<ELF>/climate/` (one shared folder per ELF) first, then in a year folder, as before.

* `buildValidationPage()` reads the observed ranks from the gates (`<key>_obsRank` of `summariseGates()`) instead of recomputing them; `observedRanks()` is removed (checked on three finished scores: the same values). `plotOverview()` and the series colours take their hues from one Okabe-Ito vector.

* `runTablesToELF()` failed with "could not find function \".\"" under `testthat::test_local()` (the module as a package does not import data.table, so data.table treated the call as data.frame code). `.datatable.aware <- TRUE` in `R/readRunTables.R`.

* `summariseGates()` also returns `<key>_obsRank` for each of its five metrics (where the observed ranks among the single runs, 0-1): the `obsRank` of G2 and G4, and for the G6 links the "observed above X% of N runs" of the detail, divided by 100. New `rankArms(scores, summary)` ranks arms by the median, over the ELFs every arm has scored, of the area skill against N1 (with pooled skill, worst area ratio and largest 10-largest ratio). New `plotObservedRanks()` draws the observed ranks, one strip per metric and one dot per ELF x arm, coloured by ELF or by the arm's fit base (`ri` arms hollow). `plotOverview()` takes an `arm` column (one row of panels per arm).

* `scoreRanking()` works with one scored year. It shuffled the observations with `sample(d$obs)`, which for a single number n is a permutation of 1:n, so a score with one `hindcastYears` stopped with "incompatible dimensions" from `cor()`. It now shuffles by index (`d$obs[sample.int(nrow(d))]`); `rho` is `NA`.

* `.inputObjects` is never cached (new parameter `.neverCache`, default `".inputObjects"`; needs SpaDES.core >= 3.2.1.9037). It reads the hindcast files on disk, which the cache key does not hold: with `.useCache` set, a score run on a folder with no finished hindcast was returned again after the hindcast had been rerun, and failed in `runWave1()` with a NULL-names error. A score now reflects the files present when it runs (the observed records keep their own cache in `<outputPath>/obs`). With no finished hindcast, `.inputObjects` stops with "no finished hindcasts in <hindcastPath>", and `runWave1()` with "no ELFs to score".

* `observedRecord()` is a `reproducible::Cache()` in `cachePath` (replacing the hand-made `cacheFile` named by its arguments), keyed on its arguments and on `fireSenseUtils::escapeFireSetDeps()`, so a record made by older escape code is no longer read back. `hindcastBurnMap()` reads the hindcast burn map once for `hindcastCore()` and `hindcastFlammable()` (which now takes it); the core is `fireSenseUtils::inELFCore()`, and `.inputObjects` checks the escape definition with `fireSenseUtils::escapeDefinitionOf()`. Needs fireSenseUtils #222.

* Scoring uses the shared escape definition and the core-start rule of fireSenseUtils (needs the `escape-core-perfire` changes: `hindcastObserved(coreStart = TRUE)`). New parameters `escapeSizeHa` (default `fireSenseUtils::fireSenseEscapeSizeHa`, 50) and `fireCauses` (default `fireSenseUtils::fireSenseFireCauses`), checked against the other modules' with `paramCheckOtherMods()` (error on a difference), passed to `readFireSenseHindcast()` (`.inputObjects` did not pass `fireCauses`) and recorded as `escapeSizeHa` and `fireCauses` on the observed record and the ELF input. The observed record counts fires that start in the core at their whole extent, with a `flammableRTM` of core and buffer (`hindcastFlammable()`); `observedRecord()` takes it as its second argument, and its cache file name has `_coreStart_<causes>_<escapeSizeHa>ha` (old `_burnable` caches are not read). Observed `escapes` are the escape set's, one per core-start escape polygon, no longer capped at the ignitions.
* `minHa` is honoured. It was passed only to the figures, while the readers and the size tests used 50: now `readHindcastSim()` counts escapes and area, `readHindcastObserved()`, `runTablesToELF()`, `scoreS4()` (through `runWave1(minHa =)`), `scoreSizesVsNull()`, `shuffledSizesAD()`, the plots and `buildValidationPage()` all take it, with no default. The parameter `minHa` now defaults to `NA`: the escape size, `escapeSizeHa`.

* The module scores fireSense hindcasts on its own: with `hindcastPath` set and no `validationELFs` supplied, `.inputObjects` reads every ELF of `hindcastPath` that has finished all `hindcastYears` with `readFireSenseHindcast()` (parameters `hindcastSets`, `skipELFs`, `inputsPath`, `ledgerSuffix`, `nCores`; observed-record caches in `<outputPath>/obs`). `init` writes `wave1.rds`, `wave1_results.csv` and `validation.html` (`pageTitle`, `pageSubtitle`, `pageDpi`) to `outputPath`. This replaces the scoring code in FireSenseTesting's `global.R`. Adds `jsonlite`, `terra` and `fireregimetools` to `reqdPkgs` (already used).

* The per-ELF panel figures are written as `panel_<ELF>` with the ELF's dots as underscores (`panel_11_3.png`): `Plots()` took `.3` of `11.3` as an extension, so 11.3 and 11.4 both wrote `panel_11.png`.

* The hindcast validation page orders ELFs by observed burn rate, most burned at the top: the scores table has a "Burned %/yr" column (the default sort, descending), the ELF picker and the first ELF shown follow it, and `elfOrder()` (so `plotOverview()` and `plotMetricPanel()`) orders by `burnRate` instead of the area ratio. `burnRate()` is the mean annual observed area (`area50`, natural fires of 50 ha or more, as the area plots) over the burnable area of the core, in % per year; `readFireSenseHindcast()` adds `burnableHa` and `burnRate` to the ELF input. An ELF input without `burnRate` sorts last.

* `observedRanks()` (the page's "obs N%" and observed-rank chart) compares the runs with the observed record of the scored years only. It summed the whole record (1986-2024) against the runs' 8 hindcast years, so the observed record ranked above nearly every run.

* The per-metric figures of `buildValidationPage()` (about 4 inches per row of three ELFs) are saved with `limitsize = FALSE`; with more than 36 ELFs ggsave refused them (over 50 inches) and the page was not written.

* Needs fireSenseUtils >= 0.2.3.9139: the observed-fire overlay of a very fragmented ELF core (5.1.2, 5.1.3) took hours per ELF before.

* `buildValidationPage()` takes `title` (default `"Hindcast validation"`), used for the browser tab and the heading; the template no longer hard-codes "burnable fits".

* `hindcastCore()` finds `rtml_<ELF>.tif` directly in `<inputsDir>/<ELF>/` (the GPSC layout) as well as in `<ELF>/NRV/370/1/`; scoring on the GPSC stopped with a missing file before.

* `runTablesToELF()` builds an ELF input from plain per-run tables (fire sizes and ignitions per run-year), for simulators that do not write FireSense hindcast folders, e.g. a fast hindcast. `buildValidationPage()` writes the scores table, the observed-rank chart, the overview and the per-metric and per-ELF figures as one self-contained HTML file from `pageTemplate.html`.

* `fireCauses` of `observedRecord()` and `readFireSenseHindcast()` now defaults to `fireSenseUtils::fireSenseFireCauses` (`c("natural", "unknown")`), the default of `fireSense_dataPrepFit` and `fireSense_summary`, instead of `NULL` (NFDB L/N, NBAC Natural), so the observed record has the causes the model was fitted to. The `"natural"` label of the observed sizes names the fires of these causes. `NULL` still gives the old record. Observed-record caches of the default now carry `_natural-unknown` in the file name, so old default caches are not read.

* Gates G2, G4 and G6 report where the observed record ranks among the single runs: `bootRuns()` also returns `single` (the statistic of each run), `obsRank` (mid-rank percentile of the observed, 1, among the runs: 0 = every run above it) and `nRuns`; `gates` gains the columns `obsRank` and `nRuns` (G2, G4; NA elsewhere), and every `detail` ratio ends "; observed above 35% of 20 runs" (G6: one per link), after the unchanged interval text. Values and pass/fail unchanged.

* `observedRecord()` and `readFireSenseHindcast()` take `fireCauses` (default `NULL`: unchanged, NFDB L/N and NBAC Natural), passed to `fireSenseUtils::hindcastObserved()`; e.g. `c("natural", "unknown")` scores the causes the model was fitted to. The groups are in the observed-record cache file's name, so records of other causes are not read. Needs fireSenseUtils with `fireCauseCodes()` (Depends-on: PredictiveEcology/fireSenseUtils#193).

* `observedRecord()` passes `flammableRTM = core` to `fireSenseUtils::hindcastObserved()`, so observed area burned and fire sizes of the unscored years are counted on the burnable cells of the core, as the scored years of the hindcast are. Its cache file name gets `_burnable` (`<ELF>_burnable.rds`), so caches of the whole-polygon count are not reused and are rebuilt. Needs fireSenseUtils#190.

* Hindcast figures come from the module: `plotAreaByYear()`, `plotAnnualAreaDensity()`, `plotFireSizes()`, `plotIgnitions()`, `plotEscapes()` (density curves; one simulated series against the observed record, medians, single-run envelopes), `plotELFPanel()` (one patchwork figure per ELF), `plotMetricPanel()` (one metric, a pane per ELF), `elfPlots()` and `plotOverview()` (the five gate ratios of all ELFs), drawn after scoring through `SpaDES.core::Plots()`, so the new `.plots` parameter decides screen or png. New `summariseGates()` gives the gate ratios as one row per ELF. New parameters `.plots`, `.plotInitialTime`, `simLabel`, `minHa`; `ggplot2`, `patchwork` and `scales` are in `reqdPkgs`.

* Gates G2, G4 and G6 report their run-to-run uncertainty: new columns `lo` and `hi` (2.5% and 97.5% of a bootstrap that resamples whole runs, with replacement, within each year, `nBoot = 1000`, `seed = 1`) beside `value` in `gates`, and "0.72 (0.63-1.03)" in `detail`. The point value and the resamples go through the same statistic (`bootRuns()`). Pass/fail logic unchanged.

* Gates G2, G4 and G6 also report the spread of single runs: new columns `runLo` and `runHi` (2.5% and 97.5% of the gate's ratio computed for each run on its own, across runs), and "0.72 (median-run 95%: 0.63-1.03; single runs 95%: 0.31-1.85)" in `detail`, so an observed record can be judged against what one run could produce. Computed in `bootRuns()` through the same statistic as `value`. `lo`, `hi` and pass/fail unchanged.

# fireSimulator_validation 0.0.0.9000

* `readHindcastSim()` no longer fails when a simulated set-year has no fires (a header-only `hindcast_sizes.csv`); that set-year reads as zero area.
* G4 compares the median of the 10 largest fires (each run's, median over runs) with the observed median of the 10 largest, not the single largest fire: one fire in 39 years was too noisy a target. The single largest fire stays in `detail` for information. New internal `topFires()`. Pass rule unchanged (within 10x).

* Wave 1 of the hindcast test plan: gates G1-G6, skill tests S1-S5, nulls N0-N2, pooled bootstrap, `runWave1()`, and a fireSense hindcast reader.
